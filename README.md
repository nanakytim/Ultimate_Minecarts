### This Mod completely overhauls Minecarts, especially the Furnace Minecart.

****
# 🚂 FEATURES
### 🔗 Linking mechanic
**- Use Iron or Copper Chains to attach minecarts together**  
**- Unlike other mods, you can use all chain variants, and copper chains loosen with oxidation.**  
**- Sneak & right-click each cart to link them. The order doesn't matter in most interaction.**  
**- Furnace minecarts will always lead the train so they cannot be attached in the middle or the back**  
### ♨️ Minecart with Blast Furnace
**- Replaces the vanilla item _Minecart with Furnace_**  
**- New model, and GUI bar to show its amount of fuel**  
**- The Furnace Minecart goes twice as fast as the other carts. Powered rails still apply to it**  
**- Use any sort of fuel to power it. Use a water bucket/bottle to remove fuel instantly**  
**- Particle and Sound effects to each interaction enhance the experience :)**  
**- Vanilla name and recipe are obviously correctly updated**  
### 🚧 FURNACE BRAKE MECHANIC
**- Right-click the Minecart with Blast Furnace without holding a fuel source  
⠀will cause it to brake and stop even if it hasn't burned all its fuel!**  
**- Right-click a stopped Furnace cart to allow it to move again.**
### 📦 Minecart with Hopper
**- Now transfers items to its parent cart, or child cart if the parent is not a storage cart**  
**- Fuel can be transfered to the Furnace cart!**  
**- Can be toggled off in config.**  
### 💥 Collision
**- Minecarts at high speed deal knockback and damage. Drive through annoying mobs!  
⠀(Can be toggled off in the config)**  
**- They scale with the amount of carts attached. A 7-cart train will kill most enemies.**  
**- Armour, enchantment and potions reduce collision damage**  
**- A fast Minecart will break a stopped one on rail, no more annoying stops!  
⠀(Can be toggled off in config)**

## ⚙️ CONFIGURATION
### **Fully server-side (no ModMenu):**

**GENERAL CONFIG**
- Toggle Linking mechanic.
- Toggle Collision Damage.
- Toggle Fast Minecarts breaking other Minecarts.
- Toggle Hopper Minecart overhaul.
- Choose Furnace Burning Time (default: 72000).
- Choose Furnace Minecart Max Speed (default: 32 blocks per second).
- Choose other Minecarts Max speed (default: 16 blocks per second).

**CHAIN CONFIG**
- Choose the Chain Resistance, the distance at which chains usually break (default: 4 blocks).
- Choose the Chain Distance for each tier (default: 1.2 | 1.6 | 2 | 2.6 | 3.2 blocks).

**FUEL CONFIG**  

Choose how much fuel each of the following sources adds or removes from the Furnace:
- Water Bucket (default: 72000)
- Water Bottle (default: 10000)
- Lava Bucket (default: 30000)
- Blaze Rod (default: 10000)
- Coal Block (default: 25000)
- Coal / Charcoal (default: 2500)
- Log / Wood (default: 2000)
- Plank (default: 500)
- Stick (default: 250)
